# Northlake Capital Partners — Pitchbook Conventions

*Fictional firm. Owner: Head of Industrials Coverage. Version 3.2, October 2026. Codified by TCS into the `northlake-pitch-conventions` skill.*

## 1. Pitch structure (sell-side, first draft)

| # | Section | Required content |
|---|---------|------------------|
| 1 | Cover | Code name, "Discussion materials", client shareholders, month and year, "Strictly private and confidential" |
| 2 | Contents | Section list |
| 3 | Situation overview | Why now, shareholder objectives, our point of view in three messages |
| 4 | Company at a glance | Revenue and EBITDA FY-2 to FY+4, end-market mix, key facts |
| 5 | Peer benchmarking | Growth and margin versus approved peers |
| 6 | Valuation summary | Football field: trading comps, precedents, DCF, LBO; illustrative range |
| 7 | Trading comparables | Peer table with medians and quartiles |
| 8 | Precedent transactions | Deal table with medians and quartiles |
| 9 | DCF | Key assumptions and WACC × terminal growth sensitivity |
| 10 | Sponsor perspective (LBO) | Leverage, returns, implied value at 20–25% IRR |
| 11 | Illustrative buyer universe | Strategics and sponsors, ranked by fit |
| 12 | Proposed process | Phases, timeline, key milestones |
| 13 | Why Northlake | Relevant credentials |
| 14 | Disclaimer | Verbatim from 10_Compliance_Disclaimers.txt |

## 2. Peer selection rules

- Six to eight trading peers. Same end markets (aerospace, medical, industrial precision components).
- Revenue between **$150M and $3B**. EBITDA margin within **8 percentage points** of the target.
- Exclude conglomerates and companies with **under 50%** of revenue from precision components.
- Every exclusion is listed with its reason in the peer selection rationale memo.
- The VP approves the peer set (Gate G1) before any valuation work.

## 3. Precedent selection rules

- Control transactions in the **last five years** (from the pitch date).
- Exclude distressed sales and minority stakes.
- Five to ten deals; show acquirer type (strategic or sponsor).

## 4. Metric definitions

- **Enterprise value (EV)** = market capitalisation (diluted) + net debt + minority interest + preferred equity.
- **Net debt** = total debt + leases − cash and equivalents.
- **EBITDA** = adjusted EBITDA as reported by the company or consensus; never mix adjusted and unadjusted in one column.
- **LTM / NTM**: never mix in one column. NTM calendarised to December year-end.
- Target NTM EBITDA = 25% × FY current-year estimate + 75% × FY next-year estimate when the LTM ends in September.

## 5. Formatting

- Currency: US$ in millions, one decimal in tables (`$870.3M`), no decimals in headlines (`$870M`).
- Multiples: one decimal with "x" (`9.8x`). Percentages: one decimal (`18.3%`).
- Fiscal years labelled `FY2025`, estimates `FY2026E`.
- Colours: Northlake Pine `#12343B` (primary), Glacier `#2E8B9A` (accent), Birch `#C9A227` (highlight, one per slide), Mist `#EEF2F1` (background panels).
- Fonts: Cambria for titles, Calibri for body; body text no smaller than 12pt in pitchbooks.
- Every chart and table carries a footnote: source, as-of date, definitions. Use "Source:" then "Note:".
- Headlines are full sentences stating the message, no trailing full stop.

## 6. Language

- No superlatives without data ("leading" requires a market-share source).
- No promissory language about outcomes or valuations; ranges are "illustrative".
- No contractions or exclamation marks.

## 7. Controls

- Any figure without a source is marked **[UNSOURCED]** and cannot appear in a released deck.
- The agent stops for approval at G1 (peer set), G2 (valuation) and G3 (release).
- The agent never sends anything to a client. The MD sends the cover note.
